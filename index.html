A LITTLE CONTEXT

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Macau , working with Prof. Chi-Man Pun . I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Hefei University of Technology , advised by Prof. Richang Hong , and was a visiting joint-training Ph.D. student at Singapore Management University with Prof. Chong-Wah Ngo .

My research connects 3D human motion perception, understanding, and embodied physical interaction . I study structured pose and motion modeling, action quality assessment, multi-person interaction understanding, and dexterous grasp generation.
